# Kepa privacy policy

_Effective date: 13 November 2026. Last updated: 13 November 2026._

Kepa helps you keep track of subscriptions, debit orders, warranties and renewals. It was built so that what you track stays with you. This policy explains, in plain language, what that means.

**The short version:** Kepa has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone. We can't see it, and nobody else can either.

## Who we are

Kepa is made by Emmanuel, an independent developer based in Nigeria and South Africa. If you have a question about this policy or your data, write to getkepainc@gmail.com.

For the purposes of South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), Kepa does not collect or process your personal information on any system we control. The information you enter is processed only by the app, on your own device, under your control.

## What Kepa stores, and where

Everything below is stored **only in Kepa's private storage on your phone**:

- **What you track:** names, amounts, currencies, billing cycles, dates, categories, colours, notes and reminder settings for your subscriptions, renewals and warranties.
- **Payment history:** the payments you mark as paid and price changes you record.
- **Receipt and slip photos** you take or choose for warranties.
- **Settings:** your country, default reminder time, appearance, and whether the app lock is on.

Kepa does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, ID number, bank login or card details, and does not need them to work.

## Things Kepa reads but doesn't keep

- **Bank statements.** When you import a statement, Kepa reads the PDF or CSV file on your phone to find repeating charges. The file is not copied into the app and nothing is uploaded. If the PDF is password-protected, the password is used only to open the file and is not saved. Only the subscriptions you choose to add are stored.
- **Receipt text.** When you scan a slip, Kepa reads its text on your phone using Google's ML Kit text recognition, which runs on the device. The photo isn't sent anywhere to be read.

## What Kepa never does

- It has no account or sign-in, and it doesn't connect you to any Kepa server. There isn't one.
- It doesn't contain advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it doesn't sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
- It doesn't link to your bank or read your SMS messages, emails or contacts.
- It doesn't access your fingerprint or face data. When the app lock is on, Android (or iOS) checks your fingerprint, face or PIN and only tells Kepa whether it succeeded.

## Permissions Kepa asks for

Kepa asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it:

| Permission | Why | When it's asked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notifications | To remind you before something renews or a warranty runs out | After you save your first item with a reminder, or during setup |
| Alarms & reminders (Android) | So reminders arrive on time rather than up to an hour late | After notifications, if you choose |
| Run at start-up (Android) | To set your reminders again after your phone restarts | Granted with the app; no prompt |
| Camera and photos | To photograph or choose a till slip for a warranty. Kepa opens your phone's own camera or photo picker, so it doesn't need its own camera or photo permission | Never; you choose a photo each time |
| Files | To choose a bank statement or a backup file to restore. Your phone's file picker gives Kepa only the file you pick | Never; you pick the file each time |
| Fingerprint / face / PIN | For the optional app lock | When you turn the app lock on |

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings. Kepa keeps working; only the feature that needs the permission stops.

## Backups

If you make a backup, Kepa creates a single file containing your tracked items, payment history, slip photos and settings, encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256). You decide where the file goes, for example Google Drive, email or a chat to yourself. Kepa doesn't upload it anywhere.

Without your password, the file can't be opened by anyone, including us. If you forget the password, the backup can't be recovered.

Kepa is kept out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so its data isn't copied to Google Drive behind the scenes. If you move to a new phone, make a backup in Kepa first (or use Android's direct phone-to-phone transfer).

## Support Kepa (tips)

Kepa is free. If you choose to leave a tip, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). We receive confirmation that a tip was made, but never your card details. Google's and Apple's privacy policies cover those payments.

## Keeping your information safe

Your information stays in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. You can add the app lock for extra protection. Backups are encrypted. Because nothing is sent to us, there is no Kepa database that could be breached.

## Your rights and choices

Under POPIA and the NDPA you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal information. With Kepa, you can do all of this yourself, at any time, in the app:

- **See and correct** anything by opening and editing it.
- **Export** everything by making a backup.
- **Delete** any item, or everything at once with **Settings → Delete all my data**. Uninstalling Kepa also removes everything it stored. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

Because we hold none of your information, we can't recover it if it's deleted, and we have nothing to hand over if asked.

If you're unhappy with how your information is handled, you can contact us at getkepainc@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) or the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## Children

Kepa is meant for adults managing their own spending and isn't directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and describe the change in the app's release notes. If a change ever affected what leaves your phone, we'd ask for your agreement in the app first.
